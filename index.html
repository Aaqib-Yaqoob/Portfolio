Executive Profile

Career built from both sides of the transaction

Aaqib's career began in people operations and multi-unit restaurant management, progressing to Head of Operations for a multi-brand Saudi restaurant business before the online channel became the priority it is today.

That operating background was followed by two years inside Meituan / Keeta during its Saudi market expansion, progressing from Key Account Manager to Senior Account Manager and then Strategic Accounts Manager. His portfolio grew from major restaurant groups — Papa John's, Mama Noura and ALFA Group — to Americana Restaurants, where he held the strategic relationship across its multi-brand portfolio: first-hand exposure to commission structures, promotions and growth planning from the platform side of relationships he now manages from the operator side.

He currently leads the delivery business for ALFA Co. / Al Faisaliah Group — a large-scale, multi-brand delivery P&L spanning 13+ brands, 84+ locations and 250+ digital storefronts — turning brand-, location- and platform-level data into pricing, promotion and investment decisions, and resetting the business from growth-at-any-cost toward profitable, self-funded growth.

Signature Case Study

From leakage to profitable growth

The Challenge

Commercial spend had built up across platforms, promotions and marketing activity without a consistent, contribution-based decision framework — funding growth without full visibility into what it was buying.

The Reset
  • Reviewed the commercial cost base line by line
  • Renegotiated aggregator economics and delivery-fee structures
  • Reallocated marketing spend toward measurable return
  • Separated organic growth from subsidy-funded growth
  • Introduced contribution-margin thinking into platform and promotional decisions
The Result
+14.5% Organic Growth +25.9% Profit Growth +7.0pp Margin Expansion −82% Subsidy Dependency

The objective was not simply to spend less. It was to make every riyal work harder.

01 · Marketplace Economics

All-in economics, not headline commission

Reframed aggregator decisions around all-in economics — commission, advertising, discount funding, delivery support, compensation and payment costs — rather than headline commission. Renegotiated commercial structures and redirected volume toward channels with stronger contribution.

02 · Performance Architecture

One view, not six spreadsheets

Built a single management view across brand, location, aggregator and month — and re-based reporting to separate organic demand from platform-funded growth, giving leadership a clear read on underlying channel health.

03 · Operational Leakage

From absolute cost to operational signal

Rebuilt compensation and refund analysis around percentage of sales rather than absolute value — isolating the locations and platform pairings driving disproportionate loss, and giving management a basis to act.
